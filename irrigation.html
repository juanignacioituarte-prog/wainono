Consent, split and pivot flows
How it works (same as the old "LH irrigation restrictions" sheet):
Allowed volume = full consent volume x allowed %. Each group (for example Manuka and River Terrace) gets its own share of that. Volume of a pivot = l/s x hours x 3.6 (m³). Hours off = 24 - hours on. Flow allowed = allowed volume / 86.4 (l/s over 24 hours).
