1The K-line

2The paddock

The paddock is cut into strips side by side. The line waters one half of a strip at a time. Give 3 points, with the GPS (stand there) or by tapping the map:
Riser
Strip 1, one end
Strip 1, other end
The two ends are the marker points at the paddock fences, in the middle of strip 1. When you set the riser and end A, the app puts end B for you, at the right distance for the line.
The map shows the strips as white lines with their numbers. Drag the round points R (riser), A and B (strip 1 ends) to move them; strip 1 moves all the strips. Drag the numbered points to move the end of one other strip.

3The order of the shifts

One line for each shift, in order. For each one, choose the strip and the side: A (the half at end A) or B. Tap a line to see that shift in orange on the map. Green = where the line is now.
    Start again with the manual order
    Strip 1 side A, strip 1 side B, strip 2 side A, and so on (own-shape sets stay at the end).

    4Corners and odd shapes, if you need them

    If a set does not fit the strips (a corner, a short strip, a bend), make a set with its own shape. You draw where the line will lie: tap the tow end first, then along the line toward the riser. Or walk it with the GPS.
    "Shape" on a set in the list lets you change that set the same way. "Copy" makes the next one beside it.

    Recorded paths (your own way)

    On the Shift page, tap "Record" before you drive. When you tap "Done", the track you drove is saved for that shift. Next time the app shows your path.
